# M07 self-trade prevention contract

`course/m07-start` preserves the historical **READY / GOAL_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** boundary on top of
`course/m06-complete`; the current source implements the **COMPLETE / PASS** target for annotated
`course/m07-complete`. M07 adds
one complexity dimension only: the matcher consumes an opaque participant group and the incoming
taker's self-trade-prevention disposition so that one positive group never trades with itself.

The matcher does not discover account relationships. Counter or an upstream policy service must
resolve those relationships before submission. Matching treats the positive group as an opaque
equality key and retains it only for deterministic admission, execution, recovery, and audit
attribution.

## Raw validation

The raw policy token is exact and case-sensitive:

```text
NONE
CANCEL_TAKER
CANCEL_MAKER
CANCEL_BOTH
```

Validation is deterministic and precedes order-book state reads:

1. M00 order fields;
2. execution-policy token;
3. participant group (`group < 0` is `INVALID_STP_GROUP_ID`);
4. STP policy token (`INVALID_STP_POLICY`);
5. group/policy pair (`INVALID_STP_INSTRUCTION`).

Only these pairs are valid:

```text
group == 0 && policy == NONE
group > 0  && policy != NONE
```

A valid raw request then follows the inherited state-dependent priority:

```text
duplicate order id
→ expected active RuleSet
→ active order-entry price band
→ M06 MarketMode admission
→ POST_ONLY raw-book / FOK STP-aware precheck
→ accept and execute
```

Invalid raw requests and business rejections consume `ApplicationSequence` exactly as their M04-M06
counterparts do, but they do not consume `AcceptanceSequence` or mutate book, registry, rule, or
mode state.

## Taker-owned disposition

Only the incoming taker's instruction decides what happens. A resting maker's stored policy is
audit state, not a command to execute later. Two orders conflict only when both carry the same
positive group. Different groups and legacy group `0` preserve ordinary price-time matching.

Every real same-group encounter emits one ordered `SelfTradePrevented` event. It includes at least:

- maker and taker acceptance sequence and order id;
- maker price and would-trade quantity;
- positive participant group and taker disposition;
- maker- and taker-canceled quantities;
- maker and taker admission RuleSet plus current execution RuleSet.

The event appears at the exact point where a Trade would otherwise appear. Non-self Trades may
therefore precede or follow `CANCEL_MAKER` events according to the real price-time scan, but no
same-group pair may emit Trade.

### CANCEL_TAKER

The maker stays unchanged. The taker's complete current remainder is canceled, the taker becomes
terminal, and scanning stops. The canceled quantity is not merely `wouldTradeQuantity`.

### CANCEL_MAKER

The maker's complete current remainder is canceled and becomes terminal. The taker remains active
and continues at the same price level, then across later price levels. It must not stop at the best
level or silently skip the self maker.

### CANCEL_BOTH

The maker and the taker's complete current remainder are canceled and scanning stops. M07 does not
implement decrement-and-cancel.

## Execution-policy composition

- **GTC**: after ordinary Trades and `CANCEL_MAKER`, a positive remaining taker may rest. A taker
  canceled by STP never rests.
- **IOC**: a non-STP remainder still emits `IOC_REMAINDER`. If STP already canceled the taker, no
  second remainder event is emitted.
- **FOK**: preflight is read-only and STP-aware. `CANCEL_TAKER` and `CANCEL_BOTH` reject with
  `FOK_NOT_FILLABLE` if a same-group maker is reached before the requested quantity is complete.
  `CANCEL_MAKER` simulates removal of self makers and accepts only when the remaining non-self
  liquidity fills the order. A failed preflight removes no maker and emits no STP event.
- **POST_ONLY**: touch or cross is decided against the raw opposite book before any STP action. It
  returns `POST_ONLY_WOULD_TAKE`; `CANCEL_MAKER` cannot first delete liquidity to make the order
  rest.

The M06 mode gate precedes both policy prechecks. A non-OPEN market returns `MARKET_NOT_OPEN`
without scanning for STP. Rule-set and mode control commands remain available under their inherited
permission matrix.

## Explicit exclusions

M07 does not implement account or parent/subaccount lookup, asset or reservation risk, local policy
hot reload, decrement-and-cancel, WAL, durable idempotency, Snapshot, networking, database state,
threads, performance layout, Aeron, replication, failover, or high availability.

The stopping point is still one deterministic in-memory `BTC-USDT` book. It can prevent a Trade
between equal opaque groups, but it cannot recover from a crash and is not production-ready.
